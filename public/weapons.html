All 8 Weapons in Forest Survival

Forest Survival hands you eight weapons and you earn seven of them mid-run by scoring points. The Pistol is yours from the first second; everything else sits behind an unlock score multiplied by your difficulty, so the same gun costs very different numbers on Easy and on Hard.

Quick answer

Forest Survival's eight weapons are, in unlock order, the Pistol, Rifle, Shotgun, SMG, Sniper, Minigun, Launcher and Subverter. Seven of the eight can aim down sights — only the Subverter cannot, because it is a hacking deck rather than a gun. Five can melee, and each weapon carries its own Mastery track from L0 to L10.

You switch with keys 1–8 or the scroll wheel, and heavier weapons slow your movement noticeably: carrying the Minigun is a different game to carrying the Pistol. If you are new, read the how to play walkthrough first.

How unlocking actually works

Forest Survival multiplies every weapon's base unlock score by a difficulty multiplier, and that product is the score you actually have to reach: Easy 1.3×, Medium 2.5×, Hard 3.5×. The Pistol has a base of 0, so it is free on every difficulty.

A worked example

Take the Subverter, the last weapon in the ladder. Its base unlock score is 2,200. On Medium the multiplier is 2.5×, so the real requirement is 2,200 × 2.5 = 5,500 score. The same weapon needs 2,860 on Easy and 7,700 on Hard. Nothing about the weapon changes — only the toll to reach it.

Every weapon, one at a time

Pistol

Forest Survival's Pistol is the sidearm every run opens with: 25 damage on a 300 ms cycle from a 12-round magazine. The Pistol is a headshot tool for the first couple of waves — accurate, quick to reload, no movement penalty. It can melee, which keeps it useful when something closes while you are dry.

Rifle

Forest Survival's Rifle is the first real upgrade, at 35 damage on a 150 ms cycle with a 30-round magazine. The Rifle is what most runs are actually won with: enough damage to drop a Normal robot fast, enough magazine for a group, light enough to keep your footwork. It fires one round per click rather than on a held trigger, so deliberate shooting beats spray.

Shotgun

Forest Survival's Shotgun deals 15 damage per pellet on an 800 ms cycle from 8 shells, which makes it a decision weapon, not a panic weapon. Point the Shotgun at something already inside your bubble — a Leaper that has just landed, or a Splitter's three children. That 800 ms gap will get you killed if you miss, so back up while it cycles.

SMG

Forest Survival's SMG is the first full-auto weapon in the ladder, at 20 damage on a 165 ms cycle from a 40-round magazine. The SMG trades per-shot damage for trigger-held uptime and a magazine deep enough to walk across several targets, which is what Horde waves ask for.

Sniper

Forest Survival's Sniper hits for 100 damage on a 1200 ms cycle from 5 rounds, and it is the only weapon with a true scoped aim-down-sights takeover. The Sniper also over-penetrates: a round punches through up to 2 additional bodies after the first, keeping 55% of its remaining damage per body. Lining a corridor of robots up so one shot passes through three is the best play it offers, and sniper-heavy Siege waves are where you want it.

Minigun

Forest Survival's Minigun fires every 50 ms for 30 damage from a 100-round magazine, the highest sustained output in the game. The Minigun is heavy and you feel it: your movement drops noticeably while it is out, so commit it to a firing position rather than running with it. It cannot melee.

Launcher

Forest Survival's Launcher holds exactly one rocket and auto-reloads the instant the tube empties, on a 2000 ms fire rate. That single-round tube is the whole design: one explosive answer, then a load. The Launcher matters most against the Overlord boss, whose per-hit damage cap of 14% of maximum health applies to bullets only — explosives are exempt. It cannot melee either.

Subverter

Forest Survival's Subverter is not a gun: it is a hacking deck that deals 0 direct damage and deploys intrusion chips from a 4-chip cartridge on an 850 ms cycle, out to 17 m. A hacked robot fights its own kind for 6.5 seconds at 2.4× its melee damage, then detonates a 6.8 m, 110-damage EMP. The Subverter cannot aim down sights but it can melee. Bosses are immune to it, and the Revenant cannot be hacked directly.

Aiming down sights

Forest Survival lets 7 of its 8 weapons aim down sights on right click; only the Subverter cannot, since it is a deploy tool with no sight line. Aiming tightens spread and steadies the weapon — most valuable on the Rifle at range, least on the Shotgun. The Sniper is the exception that changes the whole screen: the only true scoped takeover, not a moved viewmodel. On touch there is no aim button at all — the fire button auto-aims at the nearest robot first.

Melee

Forest Survival gives melee to 5 of the 8 weapons: Pistol, Rifle, Shotgun, SMG and Subverter. A swing deals 60 base damage across a 120° frontal arc out to 5.2 m, hits up to 3 targets, and has a 900 ms cooldown. That reach makes melee a real crowd answer rather than a desperation move — a swing into three robots that have crowded you beats a reload. The Sniper, Minigun and Launcher have no swing.

Active reload

Forest Survival's active reload is a timing window inside every reload, and hitting it snaps the reload to 200 ms. The sweet spot sits between 42% and 62% of the window — wide enough to learn by feel inside a wave, and a big enough saving to change fights on slow reloaders like the Minigun. Missing it costs nothing but the normal reload.

Weapon Mastery, L0 to L10

Forest Survival tracks Weapon Mastery separately for every weapon, L0 to L10 across an 11-entry XP table topping out at 3,720 XP. Every kill feeds XP to whatever you were holding, so mastery follows your habits rather than a menu choice. Because the tracks are independent, spreading kills across all eight ranks nothing up quickly — committing to two or three gets both a long way up the ladder.

Weapon FAQ

How do I unlock the Subverter?

Forest Survival unlocks the Subverter at base 2,200 multiplied by your difficulty: 2,860 on Easy, 5,500 on Medium, 7,700 on Hard, and a live figure on Adaptive between 1.2× and 3.4× base. It is last in the ladder, so you must survive deep to bank that score; kills inside an ARK-07 relay field pay up to 50% bonus score.

Which weapon is best against the Overlord boss?

Forest Survival caps bullet damage against the Overlord at 14% of its maximum health per hit, so per-shot weapons hit a ceiling. Explosives, nukes and ability bursts are exempt from that cap, which makes the Launcher and detonated barrels the efficient answer. The Overlord is also immune to the Subverter, so save your chips.

Do I keep my weapons between runs?

Forest Survival re-earns weapons every run — unlocks are scored inside the run, not stored on your account. Weapon Mastery XP does carry over, so time spent on a weapon is never lost even though the weapon itself resets. In the Tutorial every weapon is unlocked from the start, which is the fastest way to try one you have not reached.

Are all 8 weapons available in multiplayer?

Forest Survival uses the same eight weapons and the same unlock ladder in multiplayer as in solo. Some solo systems do not travel — the Mystery Box wave perks and the Tactical Director are solo only — but the guns are identical. Which class you pick and which map you play still change how each one feels; the general FAQ covers the rest.
